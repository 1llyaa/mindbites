Title: Why 16.67 mg? The maths behind our microdose

Excerpt: Three gummies is 50 mg of caffeine, six is 100 mg and nine is 150 mg. Here's why we built the dose around one very specific number.

---
<p>Most energy products give you one fixed dose: one can, one shot, one capsule. You get the whole thing or nothing. We wanted the opposite: <strong>a dose you can build up bite by bite.</strong></p>

<h2>Working backwards from the day</h2>
<p>We started with three moments people actually told us about:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>A gentle lift</strong> for a slow afternoon, around the caffeine of a small coffee.</li>
  <li><strong>A full dose</strong> for a long work session, roughly one strong espresso-based drink.</li>
  <li><strong>Maximum focus</strong> for the days that need more.</li>
</ul>
<p>Those landed at 50 mg, 100 mg and 150 mg of caffeine. The simplest unit that divides cleanly into all three, in a gummy small enough to enjoy, is <strong>16.67 mg</strong>, or one third of 50 mg.</p>

<p>Because the other actives scale with it, six gummies also deliver 80 mg of L-theanine, 333 mg of L-tyrosine and 150 mg of citicoline, plus vitamins B6 and B12.</p>

<h2>Precision is the hard part</h2>
<p>A microdose only works if every gummy carries the same amount. During development we tested how evenly the actives were distributed across each pilot batch, then tightened the process until the variation was small enough that 3, 6 or 9 always means the same thing.</p>

<p><em>Most adults should keep total daily caffeine from all sources under 400 mg. MindBites isn't suitable for children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or people sensitive to caffeine.</em></p>
