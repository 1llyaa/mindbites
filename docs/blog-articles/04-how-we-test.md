Excerpt: Before a single pouch leaves the warehouse, samples from every batch go to an independent lab. Here's what they check and why.

---
<p>A label is a promise. When a pouch says 16.67 mg of caffeine per gummy, that number has to be true for every gummy in every pouch. That's why <strong>100% of MindBites batches are lab-tested before release.</strong></p>

<h2>Step 1: Sampling</h2>
<p>We take samples from the beginning, middle and end of every production run. If the dose drifts during production, this is where we'd see it.</p>

<h2>Step 2: Potency by HPLC</h2>
<p>Samples go to an independent, accredited lab for analysis with high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC). HPLC separates a sample into its individual compounds and measures how much of each is present. For us, that means checking the caffeine and other active content per gummy against the label.</p>

<h2>Step 3: Purity and safety</h2>
<p>The same batches are screened for microbiological contamination and other safety parameters required for food supplements in the EU.</p>

<h2>Step 4: Release, or not</h2>
<p>Only batches that pass every check get released. If a batch is out of spec, it doesn't ship, no matter how close to launch day we are.</p>

<h2>Made in certified facilities</h2>
<p>MindBites is produced in the EU in GMP- and HACCP-certified facilities. Testing at the end is only half the story. The other half is a production process designed to get it right the first time.</p>
