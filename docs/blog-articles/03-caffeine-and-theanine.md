---
<p>Caffeine is the most widely used stimulant in the world, and for good reason: it helps you feel more alert. What many people don't love is how it can feel when it's on its own: a quick spike, a jittery edge, and sometimes a dip afterwards.</p>

<h2>Meet L-theanine</h2>
<p>L-theanine is an amino acid found naturally in tea leaves. It's one reason a cup of green tea feels different from a cup of coffee, even though both contain caffeine. Caffeine and L-theanine are one of the most commonly studied ingredient pairings in the nootropics space, and the pairing is popular because people describe the combination as <strong>focused but calm</strong>.</p>

<h2>Our ratio</h2>
<p>At the full dose of six gummies, MindBites contains 100 mg of caffeine and 80 mg of L-theanine. We tested several ratios during development and chose this one on two criteria:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Feel:</strong> our tasting panel rated it the smoothest of the ratios we tried.</li>
  <li><strong>Taste:</strong> it let us keep the bitterness of the caffeine in check without masking the flavour.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The supporting cast</h2>
<p>Around that core pair sit four more actives: <strong>L-tyrosine</strong>, an amino acid; <strong>citicoline</strong>; and <strong>vitamins B6 and B12</strong>. Vitamin B6 contributes to the normal function of the nervous system, and vitamin B12 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism.</p>
