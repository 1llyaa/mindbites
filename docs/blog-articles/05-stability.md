Excerpt: A gummy has to taste as good, and dose as accurately, in month twelve as it does on day one. Here's how we tested for that.

---
<p>Getting the dose right on production day is one challenge. Keeping it right for the whole shelf life is another. Gummies contain water, sugars and acids, and over time those can interact with active ingredients.</p>

<h2>Stress-testing the formula</h2>
<p>During development we stored samples from pilot batches under different conditions, including normal room temperature and warmer, more humid environments that speed up ageing. At set intervals we pulled samples and re-tested them for:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Potency:</strong> is the active content still where it should be?</li>
  <li><strong>Texture:</strong> has the gummy hardened, softened or become sticky?</li>
  <li><strong>Taste and appearance:</strong> does it still look and taste like MindBites?</li>
</ul>

<h2>What we changed</h2>
<p>Early prototypes looked great on day one and tired by week eight. Adjusting the gelling system, the acid blend and the order in which actives are added made the biggest difference. The final formula keeps its shape, flavour and dose through its full best-before date.</p>

<h2>What you can do</h2>
<p>Keep your pouch sealed, in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight. That's all it takes to keep every gummy as good as the first.</p>
